# Credits and presentation scope

KOWLO is an independent product project by Hyunjun Kim. This repository publishes a curated case study, an illustrative cover and a retained design/application comparison. It contains no private journal records or application source history.

## Published images

- `assets/portfolio-cover.png`: retained editorial cover with illustrative Hong Kong atlas artwork. It is a product concept composition, not a verified travel record.

## Geographic data

The original project records geographic sources from the [Lands Department, HKSAR Government](https://www.landsd.gov.hk/), including the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region 1:200 000 Topographic Map, and administrative boundaries from the [Home Affairs Department](https://www.had.gov.hk/) through DATA.GOV.HK. Government information remains subject to the [DATA.GOV.HK terms and conditions](https://data.gov.hk/en/terms-and-conditions) and applicable [CSDI terms](https://portal.csdi.gov.hk/csdi-webpage/doc/TNC). Information is provided as is; no government endorsement is implied. The project also records Natural Earth public-domain coastline material. Stylised atlas artwork does not establish street- or venue-level accuracy.
